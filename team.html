Cofounders

Wong Meng Weng

Computer Scientist / CEO

Meng is running the ship and the lead language architect, computer scientist, and resident good cop at Legalese. Twenty years ago, while working during the first wave of Internet startups, he created and led the anti-spam standard SPF to worldwide adoption. He's purportedly a lot wiser and more experienced now, so he wants to do the same and bring Legalese's L4 to worldwide adoption. This time, with a revenue model.

Background:

  • BSc Computer Science (U'Penn, '97)

  • Singapore Delegate to MIT's Regional Entrepreneurship Acceleration Program

  • Founder Institute Mentor

  • Entrepreneur-in-Residence at INSEAD Business School

  • Fellow at Harvard University Berkman Klein Center ('16 - '17)

  • Fellow at Stanford University CodeX ('17 - '18)

  • Co-founded pobox.com, the first commercial email service

  • Created RFC4408 SPF, the anti-spam email standard

  • Co-founded karmasphere.com, one of the first big data analytics solutions

  • Co-founded Hackerspace.SG and JFDI.Asia (Singapore's first startup accelerator)

Thomas Gorissen

Software Architect / CTO

Thomas is the conductor of agentic workers that build our products, the AI kind and soon maybe humans, too. For twenty-five years he has been pushing bits across screens with the intent to maximise user enjoyment. A Singapore transplant from Germany he grew up in the JavaScript open-source world and Southeast-Asian startup entrepreneurship. He adopted the world of rules after being uncomfortably confronted with them during his tenures at big enterprise.

Background:

  • Bachelor of Information and Communication Technologies (Fonty's, '07)

  • Founder and Organiser of JSConf.Asia (SEA's largest web developer conference)

  • Built early product for redmart.com

  • Co-creator at The Reserve thereserve.sg

  • JFDI.Asia Mentor

  • Co-founded iSyndica - World's first professional media distribution service ('09)

Michael Fairweather

Lawyer / COO

Michael is an accomplished lawyer and technology consultant that has 20 years’ experience building legal automation systems all over the world. He holds degrees in Law and Computer Science from the University of Western Australia and provided technical consulting services on behalf of Oracle and Accenture before co-founding Monad in 2010. Michael is an expert in Legal Analysis and Legislative Quality Assurance (LQA), and has been a member of the International Association of Artificial Intelligence and Law (IAAIL) since 2007. He is from Australia and resides in the Philippines.

Background:

  • BSc (IT Systems), LLB, GradDipLegPrac​

  • Founder of Monad Solutions (acquired by Legalese '26)
