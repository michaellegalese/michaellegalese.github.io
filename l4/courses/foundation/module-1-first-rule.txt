1f:T48bc,<h1>Module 1: Your first legal rule</h1>
<p>In this module, you'll write your first legal rule in L4—a simple legal obligation with conditions, deadlines, and consequences.</p>
<h2 id="learning-objectives">Learning Objectives</h2>
<p>By the end of this module, you will be able to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Write a basic legal obligation using PARTY and MUST</li>
<li>Add conditions using IF</li>
<li>Set deadlines using WITHIN</li>
<li>Define consequences using HENCE and LEST</li>
<li>Test your rule using #EVAL</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="a-simple-legal-obligation">A Simple Legal Obligation</h2>
<p>Let's start with something every lawyer understands: a simple legal obligation. Find the complete working example later in this document.</p>
<p><strong>The annual return obligation:</strong> "A registered charity must file an annual return."</p>
<p>In L4, we write this as:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">GIVEN charity IS A `Registered Charity`
`the annual return obligation` MEANS
    PARTY `the charity`
    MUST `file the annual return`
</code></pre>
<p>Let's break this down:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Code</th>
<th>Meaning</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>``GIVEN charity IS A `Registered Charity```</td>
<td>This rule applies to registered charities</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>`the annual return obligation` MEANS</code></td>
<td>The name of this rule</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>PARTY `the charity`</code></td>
<td>The charity is the one with the obligation</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>MUST</code></td>
<td>This creates a legal obligation</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>`file the annual return`</code></td>
<td>This is what they must do</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<h3 id="backtick-names">Backtick Names</h3>
<p>Notice the backticks around <code>file the annual return</code>. In L4, backticks let you use spaces and special characters in names. These are called <strong>quoted identifiers</strong>.</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">-- Without backticks, names cannot contain spaces:
return              -- a single word
-- With backticks, names read like natural language:
`file the return`         -- quoted identifier (with spaces)
`file the annual return`  -- more descriptive
</code></pre>
<p>Use backticks liberally, so your names read like natural language.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="try-it-yourself">Try It Yourself</h2>
<p>Write a rule that says "A solicitor must maintain client confidentiality."</p>
<p>Every obligation needs to know <strong>who</strong> can act and <strong>what</strong> they can do. Copy this scaffold for now—<code>DECLARE</code> is taught fully in Module 2, and <code>DEONTIC</code> (the type of obligations) in Module 6:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">DECLARE Party IS ONE OF
    `the solicitor`
    `the client`

DECLARE Action IS ONE OF
    `maintain client confidentiality`

GIVETH A DEONTIC Party Action
`the confidentiality obligation` MEANS
    -- your PARTY ... MUST ... goes here
    PARTY `the solicitor`
    MUST `maintain client confidentiality`
</code></pre>
<p>Your job: read the scaffold and make sure the <code>PARTY ... MUST ...</code> part matches the rule in plain English. Then try changing the action name or adding a second action to the <code>Action</code> list.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="adding-conditions">Adding Conditions</h2>
<p>Real legal rules have conditions. Let's add one:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">IF charity's `the status` EQUALS Active
THEN PARTY `the charity`
     MUST `file the annual return`
ELSE FULFILLED
</code></pre>
<p>The <code>IF</code> keyword adds a condition that must be true for the obligation to apply. The <code>ELSE FULFILLED</code> says: if the condition doesn't hold, there is nothing to do.</p>
<h3 id="multiple-conditions">Multiple Conditions</h3>
<p>Use <code>AND</code> and <code>OR</code> for multiple conditions:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">IF charity's `the status` EQUALS Active
   AND charity's `the annual income` > 10000
</code></pre>
<h3 id="accessing-fields">Accessing Fields</h3>
<p>The <code>'s</code> syntax accesses fields of a record:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">charity's `the status`         -- the status field of charity
charity's `the annual income`  -- the annual income field of charity
</code></pre>
<hr>
<h2 id="setting-deadlines">Setting Deadlines</h2>
<p>Legal obligations usually have deadlines. Use <code>WITHIN</code>:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">PARTY `the charity`
MUST `file the annual return`
WITHIN 60
</code></pre>
<p><code>WITHIN 60</code> means "within 60 units of time". L4 does not fix the unit; these examples count in days.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="consequences-hence-and-lest">Consequences: HENCE and LEST</h2>
<p>What happens when someone complies or doesn't comply? Use <code>HENCE</code> and <code>LEST</code>:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">PARTY `the charity`
MUST `file the annual return`
WITHIN 60
HENCE FULFILLED
LEST BREACH
</code></pre>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Keyword</th>
<th>Meaning</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><code>HENCE</code></td>
<td>What happens if they <strong>comply</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>LEST</code></td>
<td>What happens if they <strong>don't comply</strong></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<h3 id="chaining-obligations">Chaining Obligations</h3>
<p><code>HENCE</code> can trigger another obligation:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">PARTY `the seller`
MUST `deliver the goods`
WITHIN 14
HENCE
    PARTY `the buyer`
    MUST `pay the invoice` 1000
    WITHIN 30
    HENCE FULFILLED
    LEST BREACH
LEST BREACH
</code></pre>
<p>This creates a chain: if the seller delivers, the buyer must pay.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="complete-example">Complete Example</h2>
<pre><code class="language-l4-file">-- Module 1: Your First Legal Rule - Complete Examples
-- All examples are validated and use natural language identifiers

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 1: Basic Obligation (simplified for teaching)
-- =============================================================================

-- Define types needed for obligations
DECLARE `Charity Status` IS ONE OF
    Active
    Suspended
    Dissolved

DECLARE `Registered Charity`
    HAS `the name` IS A STRING
        `the status` IS A `Charity Status`
        `the annual income` IS A NUMBER

-- Define actors and actions
DECLARE Party IS ONE OF
    `the charity`
    `the buyer`
    `the seller`
    `the Commissioner`

DECLARE Action IS ONE OF
    `file the annual return`
    `deliver the goods`
    `pay the invoice` HAS amount IS A NUMBER
    `cancel the order`
    `issue a Required Steps Notice`

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 2: The Annual Return Obligation
-- =============================================================================

-- A charity's obligation to file an annual return
GIVEN charity IS A `Registered Charity`
GIVETH A DEONTIC Party Action
`the annual return obligation` MEANS
    IF charity's `the status` EQUALS Active
    THEN PARTY `the charity`
         MUST `file the annual return`
         WITHIN 60
         HENCE FULFILLED
         LEST PARTY `the Commissioner`
              MAY `issue a Required Steps Notice`
              HENCE FULFILLED
    ELSE FULFILLED

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 3: Chained Obligations - Sale Contract
-- =============================================================================

-- The seller must deliver, then the buyer must pay
GIVETH A DEONTIC Party Action
`the sale contract` MEANS
    PARTY `the seller`
    MUST `deliver the goods`
    WITHIN 14
    HENCE
        PARTY `the buyer`
        MUST `pay the invoice` 1000
        WITHIN 30
        HENCE FULFILLED
        LEST BREACH
    LEST
        PARTY `the seller`
        MAY `cancel the order`
        HENCE FULFILLED

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 4: Test Data
-- =============================================================================

`Animal Welfare Society` MEANS `Registered Charity` "Animal Welfare Society" Active 50000

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 5: Tests
-- =============================================================================

-- Test field access
#EVAL `Animal Welfare Society`'s `the name`
#EVAL `Animal Welfare Society`'s `the status` EQUALS Active
#EVAL `Animal Welfare Society`'s `the annual income` > 10000

-- Test obligation - charity files on time
#TRACE `the annual return obligation` `Animal Welfare Society` AT 0 WITH
    PARTY `the charity` DOES `file the annual return` AT 30

-- Test sale contract - happy path
#TRACE `the sale contract` AT 0 WITH
    PARTY `the seller` DOES `deliver the goods` AT 10
    PARTY `the buyer` DOES `pay the invoice` 1000 AT 25
</code></pre>
<p>Included are:</p>
<ul>
<li>Type definitions for charities, actors, and actions</li>
<li>The annual return obligation with conditions and deadlines</li>
<li>A chained sale contract</li>
<li>Test data and <code>#TRACE</code> simulations</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="understanding-giveth-a-deontic">Understanding GIVETH A DEONTIC</h3>
<p>When a function returns an obligation (not just a value), we use <code>GIVETH A DEONTIC</code>:</p>
<ul>
<li><code>GIVETH A BOOLEAN</code> - returns true/false</li>
<li><code>GIVETH A NUMBER</code> - returns a number</li>
<li><code>GIVETH A DEONTIC Actor Action</code> - returns an obligation (specifying actor and action types)</li>
</ul>
<p>Don't worry about mastering <code>DEONTIC</code> yet—copy the pattern for now; it is taught fully in Module 6.</p>

<h2 id="testing-with-eval">Testing with #EVAL</h2>
<p>Use <code>#EVAL</code> to test expressions. These examples use the <code>Animal Welfare Society</code> test charity defined in</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4-file">-- Module 1: Your First Legal Rule - Complete Examples
-- All examples are validated and use natural language identifiers

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 1: Basic Obligation (simplified for teaching)
-- =============================================================================

-- Define types needed for obligations
DECLARE `Charity Status` IS ONE OF
    Active
    Suspended
    Dissolved

DECLARE `Registered Charity`
    HAS `the name` IS A STRING
        `the status` IS A `Charity Status`
        `the annual income` IS A NUMBER

-- Define actors and actions
DECLARE Party IS ONE OF
    `the charity`
    `the buyer`
    `the seller`
    `the Commissioner`

DECLARE Action IS ONE OF
    `file the annual return`
    `deliver the goods`
    `pay the invoice` HAS amount IS A NUMBER
    `cancel the order`
    `issue a Required Steps Notice`

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 2: The Annual Return Obligation
-- =============================================================================

-- A charity's obligation to file an annual return
GIVEN charity IS A `Registered Charity`
GIVETH A DEONTIC Party Action
`the annual return obligation` MEANS
    IF charity's `the status` EQUALS Active
    THEN PARTY `the charity`
         MUST `file the annual return`
         WITHIN 60
         HENCE FULFILLED
         LEST PARTY `the Commissioner`
              MAY `issue a Required Steps Notice`
              HENCE FULFILLED
    ELSE FULFILLED

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 3: Chained Obligations - Sale Contract
-- =============================================================================

-- The seller must deliver, then the buyer must pay
GIVETH A DEONTIC Party Action
`the sale contract` MEANS
    PARTY `the seller`
    MUST `deliver the goods`
    WITHIN 14
    HENCE
        PARTY `the buyer`
        MUST `pay the invoice` 1000
        WITHIN 30
        HENCE FULFILLED
        LEST BREACH
    LEST
        PARTY `the seller`
        MAY `cancel the order`
        HENCE FULFILLED

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 4: Test Data
-- =============================================================================

`Animal Welfare Society` MEANS `Registered Charity` "Animal Welfare Society" Active 50000

-- =============================================================================
-- SECTION 5: Tests
-- =============================================================================

-- Test field access
#EVAL `Animal Welfare Society`'s `the name`
#EVAL `Animal Welfare Society`'s `the status` EQUALS Active
#EVAL `Animal Welfare Society`'s `the annual income` > 10000

-- Test obligation - charity files on time
#TRACE `the annual return obligation` `Animal Welfare Society` AT 0 WITH
    PARTY `the charity` DOES `file the annual return` AT 30

-- Test sale contract - happy path
#TRACE `the sale contract` AT 0 WITH
    PARTY `the seller` DOES `deliver the goods` AT 10
    PARTY `the buyer` DOES `pay the invoice` 1000 AT 25
</code></pre>
<p>:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">#EVAL `Animal Welfare Society`'s `the name`
-- Result: "Animal Welfare Society"

#EVAL `Animal Welfare Society`'s `the status` EQUALS Active
-- Result: TRUE
</code></pre>
<p>In VS Code with the L4 extension, hover over <code>#EVAL</code> to see the result.</p>

<h2 id="testing-with-trace">Testing with #TRACE</h2>
<p>For regulative rules (obligations), use <code>#TRACE</code> to simulate scenarios:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">#TRACE `the annual return obligation` `Animal Welfare Society` AT 0 WITH
    PARTY `the charity` DOES `file the annual return` AT 30
</code></pre>
<p>This simulates:</p>
<ul>
<li>Starting at day 0</li>
<li>The charity filing their return at day 30</li>
</ul>
<p>The result shows whether the obligation was <code>FULFILLED</code> or <code>BREACH</code>.</p>

<h2 id="common-mistakes">Common Mistakes</h2>
<h3 id="1-missing-type-declaration">1. Missing Type Declaration</h3>
<pre><code class="language-l4">-- Wrong: Type not declared
GIVEN charity IS A `Registered Charity`

-- Right: Declare the type first
DECLARE `Registered Charity`
    HAS `the name` IS A STRING
</code></pre>
<h3 id="2-wrong-field-access">2. Wrong Field Access</h3>
<pre><code class="language-l4">-- Wrong: Missing 's
IF charity `the status` EQUALS Active

-- Right: Use 's for field access
IF charity's `the status` EQUALS Active
</code></pre>
<h3 id="3-missing-backticks-for-multi-word-names">3. Missing Backticks for Multi-Word Names</h3>
<pre><code class="language-l4">-- Wrong: Spaces without backticks
MUST file the annual return

-- Right: Use backticks
MUST `file the annual return`
</code></pre>
<h2 id="exercises">Exercises</h2>
<h3 id="exercise-1-simple-obligation">Exercise 1: Simple Obligation</h3>
<p>Write an L4 rule for: "An employee must submit a timesheet every week."</p>
<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>
<pre><code class="language-l4">DECLARE `Employment Party` IS ONE OF
    `the employee`
    `the employer`

DECLARE `Employment Action` IS ONE OF
    `submit the timesheet`

GIVETH A DEONTIC `Employment Party` `Employment Action`
`the timesheet obligation` MEANS
    PARTY `the employee`
    MUST `submit the timesheet`
    WITHIN 7
    HENCE FULFILLED
    LEST BREACH

#TRACE `the timesheet obligation` AT 0 WITH
    PARTY `the employee` DOES `submit the timesheet` AT 5
</code></pre>
</details>
<h3 id="exercise-2-conditional-obligation">Exercise 2: Conditional Obligation</h3>
<p>Write an L4 rule for: "If a tenant is more than 14 days late on rent, the landlord may issue an eviction notice."</p>
<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>
<pre><code class="language-l4">DECLARE `Tenancy Party` IS ONE OF
    `the landlord`
    `the tenant`

DECLARE `Tenancy Action` IS ONE OF
    `issue an eviction notice`

GIVEN `the days the rent is late` IS A NUMBER
GIVETH A DEONTIC `Tenancy Party` `Tenancy Action`
`the eviction permission` MEANS
    IF `the days the rent is late` > 14
    THEN PARTY `the landlord`
         MAY `issue an eviction notice`
         HENCE FULFILLED
    ELSE FULFILLED

#TRACE `the eviction permission` 20 AT 0 WITH
    PARTY `the landlord` DOES `issue an eviction notice` AT 1
</code></pre>
<p>Note the <code>MAY</code>: the landlord is permitted to act, but not obliged. You'll meet <code>MAY</code> again in Module 6.</p>
</details>
<h3 id="exercise-3-chained-obligations">Exercise 3: Chained Obligations</h3>
<p>Write L4 rules for: "The seller must deliver goods within 14 days. If delivered, the buyer must pay within 30 days."</p>
<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>
<pre><code class="language-l4">DECLARE `Sale Party` IS ONE OF
    `the seller`
    `the buyer`

DECLARE `Sale Action` IS ONE OF
    `deliver the goods`
    `pay the invoice`

GIVETH A DEONTIC `Sale Party` `Sale Action`
`the sale agreement` MEANS
    PARTY `the seller`
    MUST `deliver the goods`
    WITHIN 14
    HENCE
        PARTY `the buyer`
        MUST `pay the invoice`
        WITHIN 30
        HENCE FULFILLED
        LEST BREACH
    LEST BREACH

#TRACE `the sale agreement` AT 0 WITH
    PARTY `the seller` DOES `deliver the goods` AT 10
    PARTY `the buyer` DOES `pay the invoice` AT 25
</code></pre>
</details>
