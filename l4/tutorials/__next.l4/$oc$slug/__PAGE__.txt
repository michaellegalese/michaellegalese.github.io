<ul>

<li><a href="/l4/tutorials/llm-integration/composing-l4-with-ai">Draft rules with Legalese AI</a>: turn a policy or contract into checked L4 with the AI assistant in VS Code.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="write-and-check-rules">Write and check rules</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/l4/tutorials/getting-started/testing-your-rules">Test your rules</a>: check outcomes with <code>#EVAL</code> and <code>#ASSERT</code>, and simulate obligations with <code>#TRACE</code>.</li>

<li><a href="/l4/tutorials/natural-language-functions/optimising-natural-language-generation">Make rules read like law</a>: infix and mixfix names, and rules that render as readable documents.</li>
<li><a href="/l4/tutorials/getting-started/common-patterns">Common patterns</a>: recipes for records, decisions, calculations, lists, dates and obligations.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="publish-rules">Publish rules</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/l4/tutorials/deploying-rules/exporting-rules-for-deployment">Deploy your rules</a>: mark rules with <code>@export</code>, deploy them to Legalese Cloud and call them.</li>

</ul>
