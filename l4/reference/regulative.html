Regulative rules

Regulative rules say what a party must, may or must not do, by when, and what follows if they do or do not. In L4 each such rule is a value of type DEONTIC. It decides nothing on its own: it is evaluated against a list of events, with #TRACE.

Parts of a rule

PARTY party                 -- who the rule applies to
MUST action                 -- or MAY, SHANT, MUST NOT, DO
PROVIDED condition          -- optional: a condition on the action
WITHIN deadline             -- optional: how long the party has
HENCE rule-if-complied      -- optional: what follows when the rule is complied with
LEST rule-if-not            -- optional: what follows when it is not
Keyword Meaning Page
PARTY who the rule applies to PARTY, MUST, MAY, SHANT
MUST obligation PARTY, MUST, MAY, SHANT
MAY permission PARTY, MUST, MAY, SHANT
SHANT / MUST NOT prohibition PARTY, MUST, MAY, SHANT
DO bare action PARTY, MUST, MAY, SHANT
PROVIDED condition on the action Deadlines and consequences
EXACTLY match the value of an expression Deadlines and consequences
WITHIN relative deadline Deadlines and consequences
HENCE, LEST what follows Deadlines and consequences
FULFILLED, BREACH end states Deadlines and consequences
BECAUSE reason for a breach BECAUSE
RAND, ROR rules that run side by side Deadlines and consequences

Example

DECLARE Person IS ONE OF Seller, Buyer
DECLARE Action IS ONE OF
  delivery
  payment HAS amount IS A NUMBER

GIVETH A DEONTIC Person Action
`sale contract` MEANS
  PARTY Seller
  MUST delivery
  WITHIN 3
  HENCE
    PARTY Buyer
    MUST payment 100
    WITHIN 7
    LEST BREACH BY Buyer BECAUSE "late payment"
  LEST BREACH BY Seller BECAUSE "late delivery"

#TRACE `sale contract` AT 0 WITH
  PARTY Seller DOES delivery AT 2
  PARTY Buyer DOES payment 100 AT 5

The seller delivers at 2, within 3; the buyer then has 7 from 2, and pays at 5. The result is FULFILLED.

Testing with #TRACE

#TRACE rule AT start-time WITH
  PARTY party DOES action AT time
  (`WAIT UNTIL` time)

#TRACE reads the events in order and gives FULFILLED, BREACH, or the rule still waiting for more events. A `WAIT UNTIL` event lets time pass without anyone acting. #EVAL of a rule only shows the rule itself; see #EVAL shows the rule instead of an outcome. See DEONTIC: Evaluation and EVENT.
