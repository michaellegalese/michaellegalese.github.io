3:Tdd4,<h1>jurisdiction</h1>
<p>Jurisdiction definitions for multi-jurisdictional legal rules. Uses ISO 3166-1 country codes (alpha-2, alpha-3, and numeric) and ISO 3166-2 subdivision codes. Import with <code>IMPORT jurisdiction</code>.</p>
<h2 id="constants">Constants</h2>
<p>Twenty major countries are provided with three constants each, e.g. for the United States:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">`United States alpha-2` MEANS "US"
`United States alpha-3` MEANS "USA"
`United States numeric` MEANS 840
</code></pre>
<p>Covered countries: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, China, Japan, Singapore, India, Brazil, Mexico, Switzerland, Netherlands, Sweden, New Zealand, South Korea, Italy, Spain, Hong Kong.</p>
<p>Additional constant groups (each providing <code>... code</code> and <code>... name</code> STRING constants):</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Group</th>
<th>Entries</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Supranational regions</td>
<td><code>European Union</code>, <code>ASEAN</code>, <code>NAFTA</code> (code <code>"USMCA"</code>)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>US states (ISO 3166-2:US)</td>
<td>California, New York, Texas, Florida, Illinois, Pennsylvania, Massachusetts, Washington, Delaware</td>
</tr>
