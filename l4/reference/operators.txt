1f:T1aed,<h1>Operators</h1>
<p>Operators combine values into expressions: arithmetic, comparisons and logic.
Most have a word form, which reads well in legal text, and a symbol form; the two mean the same and can be mixed. Words suit rules that lawyers read; symbols are shorter.</p>
<h2 id="pages">Pages</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/l4/reference/operators/logical">AND, OR, NOT, IMPLIES</a>: logical operators and their truth table</li>
<li><a href="/l4/reference/operators/UNLESS">UNLESS</a>: exceptions</li>
<li><a href="/l4/reference/operators/arithmetic">Arithmetic</a>: <code>PLUS</code>, <code>MINUS</code>, <code>TIMES</code>, <code>DIVIDED BY</code>, <code>MODULO</code></li>
<li><a href="/l4/reference/operators/comparisons">Comparisons</a>: <code>EQUALS</code>, <code>GREATER THAN</code>, <code>AT LEAST</code> and the others</li>
</ul>

#EVAL x PLUS y TIMES z

</code></pre>

-- Left-associative: subtraction
a MEANS 10
b MEANS 3

#EVAL a MINUS b MINUS c
-- Evaluates as: (10 - 3) - 2 = 5

-- Right-associative: list cons
#EVAL 1 FOLLOWED BY 2 FOLLOWED BY 3 FOLLOWED BY EMPTY
-- Evaluates as: 1 FOLLOWED BY (2 FOLLOWED BY (3 FOLLOWED BY EMPTY))
</code></pre>
<h2 id="asyndetic-and-and-or">Asyndetic AND and OR</h2>
<p>Legal text often lists conditions with punctuation instead of "and" or "or". L4 has two such forms:</p>
<ul>
<li><code>...</code> (three dots) means <code>AND</code>: <code>`is a citizen` ... `is over 18` ... `has ID`</code></li>
<li><code>..</code> (two dots) means <code>OR</code>: <code>approved .. pending .. `under review`</code></li>
</ul>
<p>They have the same precedence as <code>AND</code> and <code>OR</code>.</p>
<p><strong>Example file:</strong></p>
<pre><code class="language-l4-file">-- Example: Asyndetic operators (... and ..)
-- Implicit AND and OR using punctuation

citizen MEANS TRUE
over18 MEANS TRUE
`has ID` MEANS TRUE

approved MEANS TRUE
pending MEANS FALSE
review MEANS FALSE

-- Ellipsis (...) = implicit AND
eligible1 MEANS citizen ... over18 ... `has ID`
-- Equivalent to: (citizen AND over18 AND has ID)

-- Double dot (..) = implicit OR
`in progress` MEANS approved .. pending .. review
-- Equivalent to: (approved OR pending OR review)

#EVAL eligible1
#EVAL `in progress`
</code></pre>
<h2 id="other-operators">Other operators</h2>
<ul>
<li><code>FOLLOWED BY</code> adds an element to the front of a list: <code>1 FOLLOWED BY LIST 2, 3</code> is <code>LIST 1, 2, 3</code>.</li>
<li><code>'s</code> reads a field of a record: <code>`the buyer`'s age</code>. See <a href="/l4/reference/syntax#genitive">Syntax and directives: Genitive</a>.</li>
<li><code>%</code> after a number divides it by 100: <code>5%</code> is <code>0.05</code>.</li>
<li><code>AS STRING</code> converts a value to text; see <a href="/l4/reference/types/coercions">Coercions</a>.</li>
<li><code>CONCAT</code> joins text: <code>CONCAT "a", "b", "c"</code>. The <a href="/l4/reference/libraries/prelude">prelude</a> adds <code>APPEND</code> (<code>"a" APPEND "b"</code>); see <a href="/l4/reference/builtins">Built-in functions</a>.</li>
<li><code>RAND</code> and <code>ROR</code> combine obligations; see <a href="/l4/reference/regulative/consequences">Deadlines and consequences</a>.</li>
</ul>
