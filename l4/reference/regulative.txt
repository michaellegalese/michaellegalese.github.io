1f:T1475,<h1>Regulative rules</h1>
<p>Regulative rules say what a party must, may or must not do, by when, and what follows if they do or do not.
In L4 each such rule is a value of type <a href="/l4/reference/regulative/DEONTIC">DEONTIC</a>. It decides nothing on its own: it is evaluated against a list of events, with <code>#TRACE</code>.</p>
<h2 id="parts-of-a-rule">Parts of a rule</h2>
<pre><code class="language-text">PARTY party                 -- who the rule applies to
MUST action                 -- or MAY, SHANT, MUST NOT, DO
PROVIDED condition          -- optional: a condition on the action
WITHIN deadline             -- optional: how long the party has
HENCE rule-if-complied      -- optional: what follows when the rule is complied with
LEST rule-if-not            -- optional: what follows when it is not
</code></pre>

<h2 id="example">Example</h2>
<pre><code class="language-l4">DECLARE Person IS ONE OF Seller, Buyer
DECLARE Action IS ONE OF
  delivery
  payment HAS amount IS A NUMBER

GIVETH A DEONTIC Person Action
`sale contract` MEANS
  PARTY Seller
  MUST delivery
  WITHIN 3
  HENCE
    PARTY Buyer
    MUST payment 100
    WITHIN 7
    LEST BREACH BY Buyer BECAUSE "late payment"
  LEST BREACH BY Seller BECAUSE "late delivery"

#TRACE `sale contract` AT 0 WITH
  PARTY Seller DOES delivery AT 2
  PARTY Buyer DOES payment 100 AT 5
</code></pre>
<p>The seller delivers at 2, within 3; the buyer then has 7 from 2, and pays at 5. The result is <code>FULFILLED</code>.</p>
<h2 id="testing-with-trace">Testing with #TRACE</h2>
<pre><code class="language-text">#TRACE rule AT start-time WITH
  PARTY party DOES action AT time
  (`WAIT UNTIL` time)
</code></pre>
<p><code>#TRACE</code> reads the events in order and gives <code>FULFILLED</code>, <code>BREACH</code>, or the rule still waiting for more events. A <code>`WAIT UNTIL`</code> event lets time pass without anyone acting.
<code>#EVAL</code> of a rule only shows the rule itself; see <a href="/l4/reference/errors#eval-shows-the-rule-instead-of-an-outcome">#EVAL shows the rule instead of an outcome</a>.
See <a href="/l4/reference/regulative/DEONTIC#evaluation">DEONTIC: Evaluation</a> and <a href="/l4/reference/regulative/EVENT">EVENT</a>.</p>
