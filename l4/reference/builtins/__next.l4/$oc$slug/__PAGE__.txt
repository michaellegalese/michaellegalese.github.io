3:T3879,<h1>Built-in functions</h1>
<p>Built-in functions are part of the language, so they work in every file without an <code>IMPORT</code>. Functions that come from a library, such as <code>map</code>, <code>sum</code> or <code>Date</code>, are listed under <a href="/l4/reference/libraries">Standard libraries</a>.</p>
<p>Operators (<code>+</code>, <code>AND</code>, <code>EQUALS</code>, <code>UNLESS</code>, <code>FOLLOWED BY</code> and the rest) are described under <a href="/l4/reference/operators">Operators</a>. The conversions <code>TOSTRING</code>, <code>TONUMBER</code>, <code>TODATE</code>, <code>TOTIME</code>, <code>TODATETIME</code> and <code>AS STRING</code> are described under <a href="/l4/reference/types/coercions">Coercions</a>.</p>

<h2 id="looking-across-days">Looking across days</h2>
<p>These functions change the date at which an expression is evaluated. While a predicate is checked for a day, the valid time and the date the rules are in effect are both set to that day. Ranges include both ends.</p>
<p><strong>Example:</strong></p>
<pre><code class="language-l4-file">-- Temporal built-ins: looking at a predicate over a range of days,
-- and advancing contract time in a trace.
IMPORT daydate

GIVEN d IS A DATE
GIVETH A BOOLEAN
`falls on a weekend` d MEANS `is weekend` d

-- 5 to 7 January 2024 runs Friday to Sunday
#EVAL `EVER BETWEEN` (Date 5 1 2024) (Date 7 1 2024) `falls on a weekend`
#EVAL `ALWAYS BETWEEN` (Date 5 1 2024) (Date 7 1 2024) `falls on a weekend`
#EVAL `WHEN NEXT` (Date 10 1 2024) `falls on a weekend`
#EVAL `WHEN LAST` (Date 10 1 2024) `falls on a weekend`
#EVAL `VALUE AT` (Date 4 7 1776) (GIVEN d YIELD d)

-- WAIT UNTIL in a trace: the seller delivers at time 2, then nothing
-- happens until time 10. The buyer has not paid by time 5, so the
-- trace ends in a breach.
DECLARE Party IS ONE OF Seller, Buyer
DECLARE Action IS ONE OF `deliver the goods`, `pay`

GIVETH A DEONTIC Party Action
`the sale` MEANS
  PARTY Seller
  MUST `deliver the goods`
  WITHIN 5
  HENCE
    PARTY Buyer
    MUST `pay`
    WITHIN 3
    HENCE FULFILLED
    LEST BREACH
  LEST BREACH

#TRACE `the sale` AT 0 WITH
  PARTY Seller DOES `deliver the goods` AT 2
  (`WAIT UNTIL` 10)
</code></pre>
