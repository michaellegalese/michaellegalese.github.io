<p>The arithmetic operators work on <code>NUMBER</code>. Each has a word form and a symbol form, except <code>MODULO</code>, which has only the word.</p>

<p><strong>Example file:</strong></p>
<pre><code class="language-l4-file">-- Arithmetic keyword examples

-- PLUS (Addition)

#EVAL 5 PLUS 3
#EVAL 5 + 3
#EVAL 10.5 + 2.5
#EVAL (-3) + 8

-- MINUS (Subtraction)

#EVAL 10 MINUS 4
#EVAL 10 - 4
#EVAL 5 - 8
#EVAL 0 - 5

-- TIMES (Multiplication)

#EVAL 6 TIMES 7
#EVAL 6 * 7
#EVAL 3.14 * 2
#EVAL (-3) * 4

-- DIVIDED BY (Division)

#EVAL 15 DIVIDED BY 3
#EVAL 15 / 3
#EVAL 10 / 4
#EVAL 7 / 2

-- MODULO (Remainder)

#EVAL 17 MODULO 5
#EVAL 10 MODULO 3
#EVAL 8 MODULO 2

-- Operator Precedence

-- Multiplication before addition
#EVAL 2 + 3 * 4

-- Parentheses override precedence
#EVAL (2 + 3) * 4

-- Division before subtraction
#EVAL 10 - 4 / 2

-- With parentheses
#EVAL (10 - 4) / 2

-- Complex Expressions

-- Circle area: pi * r^2
GIVEN r IS A NUMBER
`area of a circle` r MEANS 3.14159 TIMES r TIMES r

#EVAL `area of a circle` 5

-- Quadratic formula discriminant: b^2 - 4ac
GIVEN a IS A NUMBER
      b IS A NUMBER
      c IS A NUMBER
discriminant a b c MEANS
  b TIMES b MINUS 4 TIMES a TIMES c

#EVAL discriminant 1 5 6

-- Practical Examples

-- Calculate tax
GIVEN price IS A NUMBER
      `tax rate` IS A NUMBER
`tax on` price `tax rate` MEANS
  price TIMES `tax rate` / 100

#EVAL `tax on` 100 8.5

-- Calculate discount
GIVEN `original price` IS A NUMBER
      `discount percent` IS A NUMBER
`discounted price` `original price` `discount percent` MEANS
  `original price` MINUS (`original price` TIMES `discount percent` / 100)

#EVAL `discounted price` 200 25

-- Combining with Comparisons

ASSUME income IS A NUMBER
ASSUME expenses IS A NUMBER

DECIDE profit IS income MINUS expenses
DECIDE `is profitable` IS profit GREATER THAN 0

#CHECK `is profitable`
</code></pre>
<h2 id="exact-numbers">Exact numbers</h2>
<p>Arithmetic is exact: <code>(1 / 3) * 3 = 1</code> and <code>0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3</code> are both TRUE.
A result that has no finite decimal form, such as <code>1 / 3</code>, is shown rounded (<code>0.3333333333333333</code>), but the value itself is exact.</p>
<p><code>MODULO</code> works on whole numbers only: <code>7 MODULO 2.5</code> stops evaluation with an error.
A negative number in an expression needs parentheses: <code>(-3) * 4</code>.</p>
<h2 id="precedence">Precedence</h2>
<p><code>TIMES</code>, <code>DIVIDED BY</code> and <code>MODULO</code> bind more tightly than <code>PLUS</code> and <code>MINUS</code>, and operators of the same level are applied from left to right:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">#EVAL 2 + 3 * 4        -- 14
#EVAL (2 + 3) * 4      -- 20
#EVAL 10 - 4 - 3       -- 3
</code></pre>
<p>The full table, with comparisons and logical operators, is in <a href="/l4/reference/operators#precedence">Operators: Precedence</a>.</p>
<h2 id="more-functions">More functions</h2>
<p>Rounding (<code>ROUND</code>, <code>FLOOR</code>, <code>CEILING</code>, <code>TRUNC</code>) and functions such as <code>SQRT</code> are <a href="/l4/reference/builtins">built-in functions</a>. The <a href="/l4/reference/libraries/math">math</a> library adds more.</p>
