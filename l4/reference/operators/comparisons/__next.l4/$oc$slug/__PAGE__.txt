3:Tf65,<h1>Comparisons</h1>
<p>The comparison operators compare two values of the same type and give a BOOLEAN.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Word</th>
<th>Symbol</th>
<th>TRUE when</th>
<th>Example</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><code>EQUALS</code></td>
<td><code>=</code></td>
<td>the values are equal</td>
<td><code>5 EQUALS 5</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>GREATER THAN</code>, <code>ABOVE</code></td>
<td><code>></code></td>
<td>the first is greater</td>
<td><code>10 GREATER THAN 5</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>LESS THAN</code>, <code>BELOW</code></td>
<td><code>&#x3C;</code></td>
<td>the first is smaller</td>
<td><code>3 BELOW 7</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>AT LEAST</code></td>
<td><code>>=</code></td>
<td>the first is greater or equal</td>
<td><code>5 AT LEAST 5</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>AT MOST</code></td>
<td><code>&#x3C;=</code></td>
<td>the first is smaller or equal</td>
<td><code>4 AT MOST 10</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>There is no "not equal" operator: write <code>NOT a EQUALS b</code>.</p>
<p><strong>Example file:</strong></p>
<pre><code class="language-l4-file">-- Comparison keyword examples

-- EQUALS

-- Numeric equality
#EVAL 5 EQUALS 5
#EVAL 5 = 5

-- String equality
#EVAL "hello" EQUALS "hello"
#EVAL "hello" = "world"

-- Boolean equality
#EVAL TRUE EQUALS TRUE
#EVAL FALSE = FALSE

-- GREATER THAN / ABOVE

-- Numeric greater than
#EVAL 10 GREATER THAN 5
#EVAL 10 > 5
#EVAL 10 ABOVE 5

-- String comparison (lexicographic)
#EVAL "b" GREATER THAN "a"
#EVAL "zebra" > "apple"

-- LESS THAN / BELOW

-- Numeric less than
#EVAL 3 LESS THAN 7
#EVAL 3 &#x3C; 7
#EVAL 3 BELOW 7

-- AT LEAST (>=)

#EVAL 5 AT LEAST 5
#EVAL 6 AT LEAST 5
#EVAL 4 AT LEAST 5

#EVAL 5 >= 5
#EVAL 6 >= 5

-- AT MOST (&#x3C;=)

#EVAL 4 AT MOST 10
#EVAL 10 AT MOST 10
#EVAL 11 AT MOST 10

#EVAL 4 &#x3C;= 10
#EVAL 10 &#x3C;= 10

-- Combined Comparisons

ASSUME age IS A NUMBER

DECIDE `is of working age` IS
      age AT LEAST 18
  AND age AT MOST 65

#CHECK `is of working age`

-- Comparison in Functions

GIVEN n IS A NUMBER
GIVETH A STRING
`classification of` n MEANS
  IF n LESS THAN 0
    THEN "negative"
    ELSE IF n EQUALS 0
           THEN "zero"
           ELSE "positive"

#EVAL `classification of` (-5)
#EVAL `classification of` 0
#EVAL `classification of` 42

-- Range Check Pattern

GIVEN val IS A NUMBER
      `minimum value` IS A NUMBER
      `maximum value` IS A NUMBER
GIVETH A BOOLEAN
`is in range` val `minimum value` `maximum value` MEANS
  val AT LEAST `minimum value` AND val AT MOST `maximum value`

#EVAL `is in range` 5 1 10
#EVAL `is in range` 15 1 10
#EVAL `is in range` 1 1 10
</code></pre>
<h2 id="types">Types</h2>
<ul>
<li><code>EQUALS</code> works on numbers, text, booleans, values of enumerations, records and lists.</li>
<li>The ordering operators work on <code>NUMBER</code>, <code>STRING</code> (in character-code order, so <code>"B" &#x3C; "a"</code>) and <code>BOOLEAN</code> (<code>FALSE</code> is less than <code>TRUE</code>).</li>
<li>Dates can be compared once the <a href="/l4/reference/libraries/daydate">daydate</a> library is imported.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="combining-comparisons">Combining comparisons</h2>
<p>Comparisons bind more tightly than <code>AND</code> and <code>OR</code>, so no parentheses are needed:</p>
<pre><code class="language-l4">GIVEN age IS A NUMBER
      income IS A NUMBER
GIVETH A BOOLEAN
`is eligible` age income MEANS
      age AT LEAST 18
  AND age AT MOST 65
  AND income GREATER THAN 30000
</code></pre>
<p>See <a href="/l4/reference/operators#precedence">Operators: Precedence</a>.</p>
