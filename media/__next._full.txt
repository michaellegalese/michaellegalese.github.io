<h2>Company Overview</h2>
<p>Marc Andreessen said, "software is eating the world"; at Legalese, we say <em>AI is eating law</em>. Our solution to the broken-law problem is to resolve it at a fundamental level, using math, computer science, and logic.</p>
<p>At the core, we are an open-source complaw project working on the drafting of legal documents the way programmers develop software.</p>
<p>We start by creating and implementing <strong>L4</strong>, a domain-specific language (DSL) for <em>legal</em> that is specifically designed to capture the particularities of law, its semantics, deontics, and logic.</p>
